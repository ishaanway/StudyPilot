Extracted Summary: Curiosity Chapter 3 (Electricity)

Key Concepts
  • Electric Current: The flow of electric charge through a conductor (e.g. copper wire).
  • Electric Circuit: A closed pathway through which current flows. Requires a cell/battery, conducting wires, a switch, and a load (like a bulb).
  • Heating Effect: When current flows, the wire heats up due to resistance. Used in irons, heaters, and safety fuses.
  • Magnetic Effect: Current creates a magnetic field around the wire. Discovered by Hans Christian Oersted. Basis for electromagnets.
Important Formulas

\[\text{Current } (I) = \frac{\text{Charge } (Q)}{\text{Time } (t)}\]

\[\text{Electromagnet strength} \propto \text{Number of turns in wire coil} \times \text{Current}\]
